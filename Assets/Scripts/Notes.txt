The difference between GameCards and InfoCards is the magnitude of information displayed.

Since the cards in the deck are too small for some detail on them to be readable, another way
of making the player aknoledge the card's effects description and other attributes has been employed: a menu.

On top of every card selected in the hand a player can press the "i" button to display a menù with a fully
rendered card, with all the information.
